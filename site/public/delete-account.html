Delete your account

This permanently deletes an MTG Event organizer account. You can also do this in the app under Settings → Shared league → Delete my account.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email, password and every signed-in phone.
  • Your place in every league you belong to.
  • Leagues you own pass to their longest-standing co-host. A league with no other organizers is deleted along with all its players, events and results.

Players don’t have accounts. To change or remove a player’s name, ask the league organizer. See the privacy policy for details.

✅

Your account has been deleted

If the app is still signed in on a phone, it will sign itself out the next time it syncs. League data saved on that phone stays on it until you remove it.
